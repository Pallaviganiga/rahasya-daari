Trekker Preparation

ESSENTIAL GEAR CHECKLIST

Pack smart and travel light! Here is everything you need inside your backpack for a comfortable weekend trek.

Footwear & Socks

  • High-grip trekking shoes with deep treads
  • 3-4 pairs of quick-dry cotton socks
  • Lightweight sandals for homestay relaxation
  • Extra polybags for wet socks/shoes

Clothing & Layers

  • 2-3 quick-dry synthetic t-shirts & trek pants
  • 1 warm fleece / jacket for cold mountain nights
  • Raincoat or waterproof poncho (Monsoon/Winter)
  • Sun cap / bandana & UV sunglasses

Hydration & Essentials

  • 2-liter reusable water bottle or hydration bladder
  • High-energy trail snacks (dry fruits, bars, glucose)
  • 30L-45L backpack with rain cover
  • Headlamp / pocket torch with spare batteries

Hygiene & Medical

  • Personal medications & pain-relief spray
  • Insect / leech repellent & sunscreen
  • Eco-friendly wet wipes & pocket sanitizer
  • Power bank (no charging points at camp base)
Safety Protocols

SAFETY & WILDERNESS ETHICS

Your safety and nature's preservation are our top non-negotiables on every single expedition.

Certified Pathfinders

Every batch is accompanied by certified trek leads with in-depth route mastery, local language fluency, and wilderness first-aid certifications.

Forest Dept. Permits

We operate 100% legally with official sanctuary entries, forest department guide authorizations, and strictly follow designated trails.

Zero Litter Policy

We carry trash collection bags on every trek. All wrappers, plastics, and debris must be brought back to basecamp for proper disposal.

Weather Precaution

In the event of severe weather warnings or swollen streams, our leads prioritize trekker safety and have contingency routes planned.

Transparent Terms

BOOKING & REFUND POLICY

Advance & Cancellation Terms

  • Confirmation Advance: The booking advance (typically ₹1,500/person) is utilized immediately to lock homestay rooms, tempo traveler seat blockings, and forest entry tickets. It is non-refundable.
  • Cancellation 7 Days Before: If you cancel 7 or more days prior to the departure date, 50% of the remaining balance is refundable.
  • Cancellation Within 3 Days: Cancellations made less than 72 hours before pickup are non-refundable as transport and logistics are locked.
100% Organizer Cancellation Refund: If Rahasya Daari cancels a trek due to unforeseen natural disasters, forest closures, or government restrictions, a 100% complete refund will be returned to you.
Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before stepping onto your first trail with us.

I am a solo traveler. Can I join your weekend treks?
Absolutely! More than 40% of our trekkers join as solo travelers (both men and women). Our trek leaders foster an inclusive, friendly environment where everyone feels like part of the Rahasya Daari tribe from day one.
Are these treks suitable for beginners and first-timers?
Yes! Trails like Kurinjal, Etthina Bhuja, and Belthangadi are very beginner-friendly. Moderately challenging treks like Netravathi, Bandaje Falls, and Kudremukha are also comfortably doable for first-timers with average fitness.
What kind of accommodation and food is provided?
We provide cozy, clean local homestays or comfortable camping tents (on a sharing basis) nestled amidst coffee/tea estates. Freshly cooked, authentic local Malnad/South Indian vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals are served.
Where are the Bangalore pickup locations?
Standard Bangalore pickup points include Shantala Silk (Majestic), Navarang (Opp. Sagar Hotel), Yashwanthpur Govardhana Theater, and KLE Dental College Goraguntepalya. Exact timings and driver contact are shared in the WhatsApp batch group 24 hours prior to departure.
Can we customize a private trek for our college or office group?
Yes! We organize dedicated custom private trips, college batches, and corporate team-building retreats on any weekday or weekend with custom pickups, menus, and itineraries. Contact us to plan a tailored package!
